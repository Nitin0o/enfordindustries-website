Water & wastewater treatment

Match treatment chemistry to the water system.

Water-treatment programmes are shaped by source-water characteristics, process design, operating conditions, discharge requirements and the performance objectives of the facility.

ENFORD's portfolio includes coagulants, flocculants, antiscalants, biocides, ion-exchange media and filtration materials. Specific product selection should follow a review of the application and relevant technical data.

  • Product and grade selection around the application
  • Specification and documentation review
  • Supplier coordination and sourcing discussions
  • Sample or trial coordination where agreed
