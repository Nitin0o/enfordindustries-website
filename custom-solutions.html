Custom development & manufacturing coordination

Define the requirement before defining the solution.

Some requirements do not fit a standard catalogue item. They need a specification brief, formulation review, target cost, process understanding and an appropriate production route.

ENFORD can help structure custom product enquiries and coordinate suitable manufacturing or processing partners where agreed. Manufacturing location, formulation ownership, quality responsibilities, documentation and commercial terms are established for each project.

  • Product and grade selection around the application
  • Specification and documentation review
  • Supplier coordination and sourcing discussions
  • Sample or trial coordination where agreed
