Food ingredients & functional systems

From ingredient selection to application fit.

Food formulations depend on more than a single ingredient. Texture, stability, processing conditions, shelf-life targets, sensory profile and commercial requirements all influence the right choice.

ENFORD brings together a broad ingredient enquiry portfolio and coordinates product information, supplier discussions, samples and application conversations around the customer's brief.

  • Product and grade selection around the application
  • Specification and documentation review
  • Supplier coordination and sourcing discussions
  • Sample or trial coordination where agreed
