Food & product consultancy

From concept and formulation to scale-up planning.

A food product is a combination of formulation, ingredient functionality, process conditions, packaging, quality, regulatory requirements and commercial viability. We help customers organise these workstreams around a clear brief.

Potential project scopes include:

  • Product ideation, benchmarking and feasibility assessment
  • New product development, reformulation and ingredient selection
  • Bakery, dairy, beverage, snack, confectionery and nutrition applications
  • Stabiliser, emulsifier, premix and functional blend development briefs
  • Process optimisation, trial planning and scale-up coordination
  • Shelf-life, stability, sensory and laboratory testing coordination
  • Packaging, label review and food regulatory coordination
  • Project reports, plant setup and equipment/process advisory
  • Cost optimisation, raw-material alternatives and sourcing strategy

How we deliver consultancy

ENFORD coordinates the right expertise for the defined scope.

The work may be delivered directly as product and sourcing support, or through separately engaged and suitably qualified independent consultants, laboratories, institutions or engineering specialists.

01 / DISCOVERY

Understand the brief

Objectives, scope, sector, constraints, expected outcome and commercial context.

02 / SCOPE

Define the work

Identify whether product sourcing, application review, specialist advice, testing or pilot work is needed.

03 / COORDINATE

Engage expertise

Agree scope, fees, responsibilities and deliverables with the customer and selected independent providers.

04 / REVIEW

Move to next stage

Review findings and coordinate product sourcing, trial or implementation next steps where agreed.

ENFORD does not claim to own or operate an accredited food testing laboratory, pilot plant or full-service engineering consultancy through this website. Specialist services are subject to scope, independent provider availability, engagement terms and any applicable approvals. No institution or consultancy is represented as an ENFORD partner unless a specific relationship is separately confirmed.
