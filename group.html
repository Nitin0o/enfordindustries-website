The ENFORD Group

A connected platform for products, applications and technical solutions.

ENFORD is being developed as a multi-industry B2B platform connecting manufacturers with ingredients, industrial chemicals, performance materials, application support and specialist consultancy coordination.

Our point of view

Better sourcing begins with understanding the application.

Manufacturers rarely need a product name alone. They need the correct grade, consistent specification, technical fit, a dependable sourcing route and clear commercial coordination.

ENFORD brings those conversations together. We work across food and nutrition ingredients, specialty and industrial chemicals, polymers, water treatment, metal treatment, coatings, construction and other process industries.

Our model is designed to grow by combining a broad product directory with customer-specific application discussions and a network of suitable suppliers and independent specialists.
