Specialist expertise for the work that needs it.

Industrial consultancy scope

Apply the same specialist model beyond food.

For industrial projects, ENFORD can define the customer's technical brief and help identify suitable independent specialists, laboratories, engineering firms or supplier technical teams. Possible disciplines include:

Water & wastewater engineering

Process review, treatment selection, dosing strategy, reuse targets and operating-cost optimisation.

Metal finishing & surface treatment

Cleaning, pretreatment, phosphating, passivation, corrosion control and line-chemistry troubleshooting.

Polymer & material engineering

Resin and additive selection, compounding, conversion-process issues and performance testing.

Coatings, adhesives & construction

Formulation briefs, rheology, dispersion, bonding, dry-mix performance and material compatibility.

Plant and process engineering

Concept design, process flow, equipment specification, project reports and technical vendor coordination.

Institutional and industrial cleaning

Surfactant systems, detergent formulation, application testing and cleaning-performance evaluation.
