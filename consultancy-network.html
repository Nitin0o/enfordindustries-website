India consultancy & technical ecosystem

Specialist expertise for the work that needs it.

A food or industrial project may need more than an ingredient supplier. It can require product formulation, laboratory analysis, shelf-life work, packaging and label review, plant planning, regulatory coordination or specialist process engineering. This selected directory helps customers identify relevant public institutions and independent providers for a project brief.

Public institutions & technical facilities

Research, analysis and pilot capabilities.

The following organisations publish services relevant to food science, product development, analytical testing, pilot work and industry advisory.

Mysuru · Karnataka

CSIR–Central Food Technological Research Institute

CSIR-CFTRI publishes industry services including food analysis and quality assurance, pilot-plant facilities, short technical-assistance projects, detailed project reports, turnkey advisory and defined R&D/product-development work.

Review CSIR-CFTRI services ↗
Kundli · Haryana

National Institute of Food Technology Entrepreneurship and Management (NIFTEM-K)

Its public research/outreach application lists formulation, recipe optimisation, sensory and consumer acceptability studies, shelf-life, packaging development, nutritional profiling, food testing, lab upgrades, equipment guidance and process-line/layout support.

Review NIFTEM-K service areas ↗
Thanjavur · Tamil Nadu

NIFTEM–Thanjavur Food Testing Laboratory

The institute describes its Food Safety and Quality Testing Laboratory as NABL-accredited and an FSSAI Referral Laboratory. Published services include analytical and microbiological testing, nutritional labelling, food-safety evaluation, trace analysis, shelf-life work, training and quality-lab setup consultancy.

Review NIFTEM-T laboratory services ↗
India · Food-sector services

National Agriculture and Food Analysis and Research Institute (NAFARI)

NAFARI publishes consultancy in product development, project identification and reports, registration and licensing, market research, plant setup, shelf-life, packaging, labelling, legal advisory, GMP/ISO implementation, FSMS audits and market linkages.

Sonipat · Haryana

Centre for Food Research & Analysis (CFRA), NIFTEM

CFRA describes its role as a specialist analytical arm supporting food safety and quality. Its published work includes analytical testing and technical support for food businesses and exporters.

Industrial consultancy disciplines

Specialist support beyond food.

For industrial projects, ENFORD can help define the application brief and identify suitable independent specialists, laboratories, engineering firms or supplier technical teams. Each engagement is scoped to the customer's requirements.

Water & wastewater engineering

Process review, treatment selection, dosing strategy, reuse targets and operating-cost optimisation.

Metal finishing & surface treatment

Cleaning, pretreatment, phosphating, passivation, corrosion control and line-chemistry troubleshooting.

Polymer & material engineering

Resin and additive selection, compounding, conversion-process issues and performance testing.

Coatings, adhesives & construction

Formulation briefs, rheology, dispersion, bonding, dry-mix performance and material compatibility.

Plant and process engineering

Concept design, process flow, equipment specification, project reports and technical vendor coordination.

Institutional and industrial cleaning

Surfactant systems, detergent formulation, application testing and cleaning-performance evaluation.
