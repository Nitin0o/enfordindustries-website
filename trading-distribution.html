Trading · distribution · sourcing

A practical sourcing partner for multi-industry requirements.

Manufacturers need reliable access to suitable materials, clear product information and commercial coordination. ENFORD's trading and distribution model is designed to connect customer requirements with appropriate suppliers and product options.

Our enquiry scope spans ingredients, vitamins and minerals, nutraceutical components, specialty chemicals, water-treatment materials, polymers and process inputs. Each requirement is reviewed for grade, specification, documentation, source, packaging, regulatory context and availability before a commercial commitment is made.

  • Domestic B2B trading and distribution enquiries
  • Supplier discovery and sourcing coordination
  • Import and export enquiries, subject to product and destination requirements
  • Specification, documentation and commercial coordination
