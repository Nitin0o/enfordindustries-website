How application support begins

Bring the process details, not only the product name.

A useful technical brief tells us what is being made, how it is processed, what the material must do and what currently needs improvement. The first conversation helps determine whether the next step is a product alternative, a sample, a supplier discussion or specialist technical work.

  • Application and end-product description
  • Current grade, specification or formulation constraints
  • Equipment, process conditions and operating range
  • Target performance, quality issue or cost objective
  • Quantity, location and decision timeline
