About ENFORD

A sourcing-led partner for ingredients and industrial materials.

ENFORD is being developed around a broad B2B product network, connecting manufacturers with ingredients, chemicals and materials matched to their requirements.

Our approach

We begin with the customer's product requirement: name, grade, specification, application, quantity and delivery location. ENFORD reviews the brief and coordinates sourcing discussions with suitable suppliers, subject to technical fit and availability.

The published product directory is a sourcing enquiry catalogue. It should not be read as a statement of current inventory, owned manufacturing capacity, certifications or supplier appointments.

Our product scope

Food ingredients, nutraceutical materials, specialty chemicals, polymers, water treatment, metal treatment, coatings, construction and other industrial product categories.
