Technical resources

Help us understand the requirement behind the product.

A useful technical brief helps narrow the grade, application and sourcing route. Use the checklist below before sending an enquiry.

01

Product and specification

Product name, grade, purity/concentration, mesh/viscosity or other required technical characteristics. Attach a TDS or sample specification where possible.

02

Application and process

End use, formulation or process description, substrate/material, equipment and relevant operating conditions.

03

Target performance

What must improve or be achieved—texture, stability, cleaning, corrosion protection, process efficiency, cost or another measurable outcome.

04

Quantity and delivery

Sample need, trial quantity, monthly/annual volume, packaging preference and delivery city/state.

05

Compliance and documentation

Required COA, SDS, food-grade or other regulatory status, customer approvals and applicable market requirements.

06

Timing and commercial range

Trial or project timeline, target landed cost where known, incumbent material and current challenges.

Do not share confidential formulations, proprietary process documents or sensitive information in a first message. Agree a suitable confidentiality process before sharing protected information.
