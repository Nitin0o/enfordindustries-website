Nutrition, nutraceuticals & premixes

A portfolio for nutrition-led product development.

Nutrition products require careful attention to nutrient form, potency, compatibility, dosage format, documentation and intended application. Ingredient selection starts with the product brief and its technical requirements.

ENFORD's enquiry portfolio spans vitamins and minerals, protein ingredients, amino acids, selected nutraceutical actives and custom premix discussions. Exact grade, specification and regulatory documentation are reviewed for each requirement.

  • Product and grade selection around the application
  • Specification and documentation review
  • Supplier coordination and sourcing discussions
  • Sample or trial coordination where agreed
