Metal finishing, aluminium & foundry

Support for the steps behind a finished metal surface.

Metal-treatment requirements vary by substrate, soil load, bath conditions, process sequence, finish specification and downstream coating or assembly needs.

ENFORD welcomes B2B enquiries for degreasing, pickling, phosphating, passivation, foundry auxiliaries and related process materials. Product choice and process recommendations require a review of the actual line and operating conditions.

  • Product and grade selection around the application
  • Specification and documentation review
  • Supplier coordination and sourcing discussions
  • Sample or trial coordination where agreed
