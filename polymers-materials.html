Polymers, resins & performance materials

A materials portfolio built around end-use performance.

Materials are selected against processing conditions, substrate compatibility, mechanical and surface requirements, regulatory context and the customer's commercial brief.

ENFORD's enquiry scope spans polymers and resins, plastics additives, pigments and dispersants, construction additives, minerals and selected elastomer chemicals. Availability and technical fit are confirmed case by case.

  • Product and grade selection around the application
  • Specification and documentation review
  • Supplier coordination and sourcing discussions
  • Sample or trial coordination where agreed
